# Input: Shared Capability

## Screen: Product Reviews
Under each product page, a list of star ratings + written reviews from
other customers. A "Write a review" button for verified purchasers.

## Screen: Seller Ratings
On a seller's profile page, an aggregate star rating and a list of
buyer comments about that seller, separate from product reviews.

## Screen: Customer Support Ticket
When a user submits a support ticket, they can optionally attach a star
rating of "how was this resolved" once the ticket closes.

## Note
All three of the above use the same underlying "rating + written comment"
concept, just attached to different things (a product, a seller, a
support interaction).
